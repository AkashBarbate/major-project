CHAPTER 8: RESULT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
===========================================

8.1 Introduction
-----------------

This chapter presents the experimental results of the NewsLens system across its three main components: multi-label classification, threshold optimization, and credibility scoring. We also analyze the model's behavior through XAI attributions and error analysis. All metrics reported here are computed on a held-out validation set that was not seen during training.


8.2 Evaluation Metrics
------------------------

We use five metrics to evaluate the classification model. Each captures a different aspect of multi-label performance.

Precision measures what fraction of the labels the model predicted are actually correct. If the model predicts three labels for an article and two of them are right, the precision is 0.67.

Recall measures what fraction of the true labels the model managed to find. If an article has three true labels and the model predicts two of them, the recall is 0.67.

F1-Score is the harmonic mean of precision and recall, providing a single balanced measure that penalizes models that sacrifice one metric for the other. We report both macro F1 (averaging across categories equally) and weighted F1 (weighted by category support).

Accuracy in the multi-label context is subset accuracy: the fraction of articles where the model predicts the exact set of labels correctly. This is a strict metric because even getting one label wrong counts as a miss.

Hamming Loss measures the fraction of individual label predictions that are wrong, averaged across all articles and all labels. Unlike accuracy, it gives partial credit for partially correct predictions.


8.3 Experimental Setup
------------------------

The dataset was split into 80 percent training and 20 percent validation. The baseline model (DistilBERT with 66M parameters) was trained for 5 epochs with a learning rate of 2e-5 and batch size of 16. The improved model (RoBERTa-base with 125M parameters) used the same hyperparameters on the custom dataset. All experiments were conducted using PyTorch on a GPU-enabled system.

8.7 Calibration Results
--------------------------

After threshold optimization, we applied temperature calibration to improve the reliability of the model's probability outputs. Temperature scaling divides the logits by a learned temperature parameter (T = 1.42 in our case) before applying the sigmoid function, which smooths out overconfident predictions.

>>> INSERT FIGURE HERE <<<
Figure 8.8: Reliability Diagrams Before and After Calibration

>>> INSERT FIGURE HERE <<<

The Expected Calibration Error dropped from 0.0823 to 0.0312 after calibration, a 62 percent improvement. This means that when the model says it is 80 percent confident about a prediction, the actual accuracy is now much closer to 80 percent than it was before calibration.

8.11 Error Analysis
---------------------

>>> INSERT FIGURE HERE <<<
Figure 8.17: Category-wise Error Analysis (TP, FP, FN)
File: charts/output/chart_10_5_error_analysis.png

The error analysis reveals that most false positives occur in categories with high semantic overlap. International Affairs and Politics share the highest false positive rate because articles about government foreign policy naturally contain vocabulary from both domains. Similarly, Science and Technology occasionally generates false positives for Health when articles discuss medical research.

False negatives are most common in Education, which is the smallest and most diverse category. Education-related articles vary widely in topic, from school policies to university research to student programs, making it harder for the model to learn a consistent pattern.


8.12 System Performance
--------------------------

The complete inference pipeline (tokenization + model forward pass + credibility computation + XAI attribution) takes approximately 1.8 seconds per article on CPU and under 0.5 seconds on GPU. The FastAPI backend handles concurrent requests using async processing, and the Streamlit frontend provides immediate visual feedback while the backend processes the request.


8.13 Summary
--------------

The results demonstrate that the NewsLens system performs effectively across all three of its core tasks. Multi-label classification achieves a macro F1 of 0.91 with per-category thresholds optimized through Optuna. Temperature calibration improves probability reliability by 62 percent. The credibility scoring module produces meaningful and explainable scores, and the XAI module confirms that the model's decisions are grounded in semantically relevant features.
