SLIDE 3 — LITERATURE REVIEW
=====================================================================
Layout: Two-column (text left, table right)

Title: Background & Literature Review

Left side (bullet points):
  • Traditional ML (Naive Bayes, SVM, Logistic Regression)
    — relies on TF-IDF, ignores word order and context
  • Deep Learning (CNN, LSTM) — learns features automatically
    but struggles with long-range dependencies
  • Transformers (BERT, RoBERTa) — self-attention captures
    full context; pre-trained on massive corpora
  • We chose RoBERTa-base: outperforms DistilBERT by
    +8% macro F1 on our dataset
  • Gap: no system combines multi-label classification +
    credibility + XAI in a single pipeline

=====================================================================
Layout: Two-column (text left, visuals right — stacked)

Title: Proposed Methodology — Data Pipeline

Left side (bullet points):
  • Scraped 5,248 articles from RSS, CC-News, web scraping

SLIDE 6 — METHODOLOGY: MODEL TRAINING
=====================================================================
Layout: Two-column (text left, figure right)

Title: Proposed Methodology — Model Training

Left side (bullet points):
  • RoBERTa-base (125M params) fine-tuned for multi-label
  • Sigmoid activation + BCE loss (independent per label)
  • Training: 5 epochs, lr=2e-5, batch=16, AdamW optimizer
  • Baseline (DistilBERT): F1 = 0.8428
  • Improved (RoBERTa + Optuna): F1 = 0.91
  • Temperature calibration: ECE reduced by 62%

SLIDE 7 — METHODOLOGY: CREDIBILITY & XAI
=====================================================================
Layout: Two-column (text left, figure right)

Title: Credibility Scoring & Explainable AI

Left side (bullet points):
  • 8 linguistic features extracted per article:
    subjectivity, CAPS ratio, exclamation density,
    clickbait signals, sensational vocab, article length,
    headline questions, source reputation
  • Weighted formula → score 0-100
  • XAI via Integrated Gradients (transformers-interpret)
  • Per-word attribution for each predicted label
  • Fully transparent — user sees WHY each label was assigned
